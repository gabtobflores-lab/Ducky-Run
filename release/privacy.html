Mirror Drop

Privacy policy and support

No data collected

Mirror Drop does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game itself never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your nickname, profile picture, best scores and game settings are saved only on your own iPhone so they are there next time you play. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are sent to Game Center so they can appear on worldwide leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we never receive your Apple ID, name or any other information. Game Center is optional.

Children

Because no information is collected from anyone, Mirror Drop is safe for players of all ages.
