# 15 FLOW Business Ideas

Each idea is scored 1–10 on 11 criteria, with 'Low cost' and 'Teen-able' scored so higher is better. The FLOW score weights earning power: profit ×2, demand and long-term ×1.5, scalability ×1.3, recurring ×1.2, speed ×0.8, the rest ×1. Earnings ranges are estimates for a part-time teen operator with parental help; verify local prices.

1. **Recruit Reel** (Original, FLOW score 7.97): College-recruiting highlight films and athlete profiles for high-school athletes, filmed at their games and delivered in 72 hours. Pricing: Reel $249 · Reel + profile + coach-email kit $449 · Season plan (4 updates) $799. Start-up: $350–$700: auto-tracking phone gimbal, tripod, CapCut/DaVinci (free), a $12/yr domain, Google Forms. Year 1: $8K–$30K (20–60 families). Word of mouth inside teams is powerful because every reel is visible to the whole roster..
2. **Ref Ready** (Original, FLOW score 7.77): A referee staffing service for youth rec leagues: certified teen and adult refs booked, scheduled and paid through one simple system. Pricing: League pays the normal ref fee + a $5–$8 booking fee per game slot · Tournament packages. Start-up: $100–$400 (your certification, a scheduling tool, a simple site). Year 1: $6K–$20K, more if you referee too.
3. **Glow Holiday Lights** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 7.59): Design, install, take down and store holiday lights for homes and small businesses: one booking, zero ladders for the customer. Pricing: Average home $900–$2,000 (lights included year 1) · Renewal ~60% of year 1 · Storage $50/yr. Start-up: $1,500–$3,000: ladders, safety harness, starter light inventory, insurance (parent-held). Year 1: $15K–$40K in a 10-week season with 15–30 homes.
4. **Scoop Squad** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 7.38): Weekly dog-waste removal subscriptions: boring, unglamorous and very profitable. Pricing: $15–$25 per weekly visit for 1–2 dogs. Start-up: $150–$400. Year 1: $8K–$25K recurring.
5. **Slab Club** (Original, FLOW score 7.3): A trading-card grading concierge: collectors drop off cards; you pre-screen, batch-submit to PSA/SGC/BGS at bulk rates and return slabbed cards. Pricing: $10–$15 per card on top of the grading fee · Pre-screen only $2/card · Sell-on-consignment option 10%. Start-up: $200–$500: loupe, light box, sleeves/top-loaders, a grading-company membership, shipping insurance. Year 1: $6K–$25K depending on volume.
6. **SetUp Concierge** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 7.26): Tech setup and monthly check-ins for seniors: phones, TVs, smart doorbells, video calls, scam protection. Pricing: $60 setup visit · $35–$50/month membership · $200 community session. Start-up: $50–$200. Year 1: $6K–$18K.
7. **Curbside** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 7.11): Weekly trash-bin valet plus monthly bin washing: bins out to the curb, back in, and smelling clean, on subscription. Pricing: Valet $20–$30/month · Washing $15/month · HOA bulk contracts. Start-up: $300–$900 (pressure washer, deodorizer, door hangers). Year 1: $6K–$15K recurring.
8. **Gear Library** (Original, FLOW score 6.95): A size-up subscription for kids' sports equipment: hockey, lacrosse, skates and cleats swapped for the next size every season. Pricing: $15–$30 per kid per month, or $120/season. Start-up: $800–$2,000 starter inventory (largely used gear). Year 1: $5K–$18K.
9. **Yard Card** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 6.71): Overnight celebration lawn signs for birthdays, graduations, signing days and "welcome home": set up before sunrise, gone in 24 hours. Pricing: $95–$175 per display · School senior-sign packages $25/sign × class size. Start-up: $600–$1,200 for the first letter/graphic set and stakes. Year 1: $5K–$20K; graduation season (May–June) is huge.
10. **Formal Closet** (Original, FLOW score 6.56): A rental closet for prom, homecoming and winter formal: dresses, suits and accessories at 20% of retail, with fitting nights at your house. Pricing: $40–$90 per rental + $25 cleaning · Accessories $10–$20. Start-up: $800–$2,000 inventory + steamer and racks. Year 1: $5K–$15K.
11. **FLOW Edge** (Original, FLOW score 6.5): A skate-sharpening and gear-freshening subscription for youth hockey families, picked up after practice and returned before the next one. Pricing: $10/sharpen · $35/month family plan · $120/month team plan. Start-up: $1,200–$1,800. Year 1: $4K–$12K.
12. **Snapbooth** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 6.35): A sleek iPad photo booth for parties, proms, bar mitzvahs and corporate events, with instant texted GIFs and branded prints. Pricing: $250–$450 for 3 hours · Prints +$150 · Custom backdrop +$75. Start-up: $900–$2,000 (iPad you may already own, stand, light, backdrop, props). Year 1: $8K–$25K (25–60 events).
13. **Move-Out Rescue** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 6.31): Collect furniture students abandon at college move-out (free or cheap) and resell it to incoming students at move-in, delivered. Pricing: Bundles $150–$400 · Mini-fridges $60–$90 · Delivery fee $40. Start-up: $600–$1,500 (truck rental, storage unit). Year 1: $5K–$20K per cycle.
14. **Memory Lane** (Proven money-maker, FLOW score 6.28): Local pickup of old photos, slides and home videos, digitized, organized by year and delivered in a private online album plus a USB drive. Pricing: $0.40–$0.60 per photo · $25–$35 per tape · Memorial slideshow $150. Start-up: $700–$1,500 (fast photo scanner, capture device, VCR, storage). Year 1: $8K–$30K.
15. **Tourney Base** (Original, FLOW score 6.15): A tournament-day basecamp for youth sports families: canopy, chairs, cooler, charging and snacks set up before the first game. Pricing: $300–$500 per team per tournament day · Snack add-ons. Start-up: $1,000–$2,500 (canopies, chairs, coolers, cart). Year 1: $6K–$20K.
