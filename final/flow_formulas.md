# FLOW Formulas
**FLOW EXTREME** = 0.25·Growth + 0.25·Optionality + 0.20·Momentum + 0.15·ExpectationGap + 0.15·Catalyst, gated on BalanceSheet ≥ 5. Rules: top 8, capped at 20% each, exit at −35%. Expected max drawdown is −55% to −80%. It has no stock-level out-of-sample test. Its rank correlates 0.64 with momentum.

**FLOW CORE** = 0.30·Quality + 0.20·BalanceSheet + 0.15·Valuation + 0.15·LowVol + 0.10·Growth + 0.10·Momentum, plus a market-trend overlay (50% equity when the S&P is below its 10-month SMA). Only the overlay is tested out-of-sample.

The two rankings correlate at −0.27 and share 2 of their top-10 names. Top-10 stability under ±50% weight noise is 94% for EXTREME and 97% for CORE.
