# FLOW Investing Framework: FLOW DIVERGENCE (Rejected)
**Principle:** buy when price stress diverges from improving fundamentals, and exit when both break.
**Result:** 86% of its positions matched the plain 10-month trend rule. Out-of-sample Sharpe was 0.83 vs 0.84 for trend. The core conditional relationship flipped between 1900–79 and 1980–2023. v2, gated on the equity risk premium, did worse (0.75).
**Verdict:** rejected as a new edge at index level. **Next experiment:** a stock-level version using point-in-time analyst estimate revisions vs price drawdown.
