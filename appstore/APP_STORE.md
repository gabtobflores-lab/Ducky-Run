## Description (4000 max)

> **Two balls. One tap. Zero Wi-Fi needed.**
>
> Mirror Drop is a calm-looking, fast-playing arcade game you can take anywhere. Every tap moves both balls at once, mirrored across the screen. Squeeze them together, spread them apart, and thread them through the falling blocks. Grab gems to build your combo. Every ten seconds the world speeds up.
>
> **A sky that's alive**
> The weather changes while you play, and each event looks and feels different:
> • **Aurora**: shimmering northern lights drift and fold overhead
> • **Storm**: heavy clouds, rain on the glass, and lightning that can charge the blocks
> • **Meteor shower**: a violet night sky with a real moon and shooting stars
> • **Fog**: the screen clouds over, so keep your nerve
> • **Hail**: short and heavy
> • **Wind**: gusts push both balls off course
> • **Heat wave** and **sun flash**: shimmering air and blinding glare
>
> **Play anywhere**
> Mirror Drop never needs an internet connection. Play on a plane, on the subway, in the mountains or in airplane mode. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Pay once and it's yours.
>
> **Your pace**
> • Six difficulty levels, from Chill to Impossible
> • Normal mode with a final stretch to finish, or Infinite mode for high-score runs
> • Two or three columns per side
> • A best score for every combination
>
> **Made with care**
> Smooth, battery-friendly graphics tuned for iPhone, gentle animations that respect Reduce Motion, and a game that pauses itself the moment you leave.
>
> One tap to learn. One more run, every time.

## App Review notes

> Mirror Drop is a single-player arcade game that runs entirely on the device. It needs no network connection, account or permissions, and it collects no data. Tap anywhere to move both balls; weather events appear at random during play.
