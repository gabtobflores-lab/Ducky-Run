HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • W↑Switch to upper path
  • S↓Switch to lower path
  • SpaceJump — hold for extra height
  • J⇧Dash attack — smashes enemies
  • EDuck ability
  • PEscPause
  • MMute audio

On touch: swipe up / down to change path, tap to jump, swipe right to dash.

Field Guide

  • Propeller Hats — the classic duck currency. Chain them for a score multiplier.
  • Eggs — rare and precious. Worth big points and they unlock ducks.
  • Feather Shield — soaks one hit.
  • Bread Magnet — pulls in every pickup nearby.
  • Turbo Quack — speed burst, invincible while it lasts.
  • Armoured foes (crabs, shells) shrug off dashes — stomp them from above.
