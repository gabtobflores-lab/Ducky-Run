MIRROR DROP

privacy policy · effective October 20, 2026

The short version

Mirror Drop doesn't collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your best scores, settings, profile name, colour and any profile photo you choose are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. A photo you pick is cropped on the device and never leaves it.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and the game shows your Game Center name and picture. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or keep this information.

Purchases

Packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles all payment details; we never see your name, payment method or Apple ID. The game only learns which packs you own, so it can unlock them.

Sharing

When you share a score, the iPhone's share sheet sends the picture and text only where you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.

Children

Mirror Drop is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: gabtobflores@gmail.com.
